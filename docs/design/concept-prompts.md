# Concept image prompts (design step, draft)

Status: draft, waiting on Jake's answers to design round 1. Nothing has been generated yet.

These prompts cover four style directions, with two images each. Every image shows the same first-person moment, so the directions can be compared directly:
- a chunky gun held in the right hand;
- a wall the player just built, up close on the left;
- the enemy about 10 m away, mid-hit;
- the magical far vista behind.

Near things are cartoon. Far things are "Harry Potter × Star Wars" magical.

## Shared base (prepended to each prompt)

> First-person video game screenshot, 16:9. Right hand holds a chunky stylized blaster rifle shaped like a Fortnite assault rifle, with glowing magical parts: rune-etched crystal core, brass fittings, energy cell. The weapon fires a bright spell bolt with a sparkling trail. On the left, up close, stands a player-built wall panel. In the middle distance, about 10 meters away, a cartoon enemy is mid-hit, with a burst of magical sparks and a floating damage number. Clean game HUD: health and shield bars bottom-left, ammo bottom-right, hotbar bottom-center. No text other than the HUD numbers.

## A. Looney Tunes Wizard Wars

1. Bold black ink outlines, squash-and-stretch cartoon shapes, saturated primaries. The wall is warped cartoon wooden planks. The enemy is a goofy robed wizard-droid with a big round helmet. Far vista: floating castle islands with waterfalls pouring into a purple starry nebula, a ringed planet, and a tiny starship streaking past.
2. The same style at sunset. The enemy is a cartoon stormtrooper-style knight with a pointed wizard hat. Far vista: a huge moon over twisting castle spires, with glowing lanterns on rope bridges.

## B. Galactic Hogwarts

1. Cel-shaded with soft painterly lighting. The wall is mossy enchanted stone blocks with glowing runes. The enemy is a cartoon armored battle-mage. Far vista: a gothic castle built on a drifting asteroid, twin suns, and starships docking at tower tops.
2. The same style at night. Aurora curtains, candle-lit castle windows, and a star-destroyer-shaped cloud of glowing dust.

## C. Family Guy flat

1. Flat colors, thin clean outlines, simple two-tone shading, a TV-cartoon look. The wall is a flat cartoon brick panel. The enemy is a round, big-eyed cartoon trooper. Far vista: a sky full of glowing rune constellations, a giant cratered moon, and floating lanterns.
2. The same style by day. Pastel sky, a floating wizard school on a cloud, and a pair of X-wing-like broomstick fighters.

## D. Arcane Space Academy

1. Fortnite-like cartoon proportions with a glossy stylized finish. The wall is a crystal-reinforced metal panel. The enemy is a stylized robot duelist with a floating spellbook. Far vista: a cathedral space station with stained-glass solar sails, over a swirling galaxy.
2. The same style at dusk. A spellcasting arena ring floats in space, with portals glowing on the rim and ships silhouetted against the nebula.

## Notes

- Nothing will be copied from real franchises. "Harry Potter × Star Wars" and "Family Guy / Looney Tunes" describe the mood, and the prompts avoid named characters, logos and copyrighted designs.
- Jake picks one direction, or a mix. Everything after that goes into the chosen style.
